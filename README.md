# Hi, I’m Pam 👋

**Architecture × Visual Storytelling × Web**

I’m an architectural designer at **Lead8** in Hong Kong. I work on spaces, make architectural pitch films, and design and build for the web.

Architecture is the foundation of how I think: how things fit together, how people move through them, and how the smallest detail affects the whole. That curiosity follows me into film, code, and a growing collection of side projects.

Sometimes the output is a building. Sometimes it’s a website. Sometimes it’s an unnecessarily elaborate way to study French.

## Fresh out of the editor 🚀

I just (re)launched **[pamelamaguigad.com](https://pamelamaguigad.com)**, a major overhaul of my personal website and digital garden, now fully bilingual in **English and French**.

I brought the visual identity, copy, design, and development together myself. Built with **[Astro](https://astro.build/)**, **TypeScript**, **[Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/)**, **[GSAP](https://gsap.com/)**, and **[Lenis](https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis)**, featuring build-time content validation and plenty of attention to motion.

The garden is where I share working notes on design, code, cities, and learning. Some are polished. Some are still figuring themselves out. That’s the point.

## Currently down these rabbit holes 🐇

- **Cities as networks.** Exploring Bangkok’s street topology with Python and **[OSMnx](https://osmnx.readthedocs.io/)**, working towards an interactive urban network analysis app.
- **French, with extra tooling.** Experimenting with Astro, **[AnkiConnect](https://foosoft.net/projects/anki-connect/)**, and TypeScript to connect learning notes and spaced repetition. Working from B2 towards C1.
- **A reference library I’ll actually remember.** Exploring architectural precedent databases, visual references, and active recall.
- **Tools for thought.** Connecting notes, refining workflows, and occasionally building a tool to solve a problem created by another tool.
